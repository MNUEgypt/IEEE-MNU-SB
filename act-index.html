PES Day

IEEE PES Day at Mansoura National University is a remarkable event organized by the IEEE MNU Student Branch in collaboration with the IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES) Egypt Section. This annual celebration highlights achievements and innovations in the power and energy field. The event features expert speakers, engaging technical sessions, and hands-on workshops on topics such as renewable energy, smart grids, and electric mobility. It serves as a platform for students and professionals to exchange knowledge, foster community engagement, and promote professional growth within the energy sector.

At IEEE MNU Student Branch, we believe that learning goes beyond the classroom. Our activities are designed to inspire innovation, leadership, and collaboration among students. Throughout the year, we organize a variety of technical sessions, industrial tours, and engaging events that connect students with real-world applications of engineering and technology. From insightful workshops and hands-on training to inspiring company visits and community projects, every activity reflects our mission to empower future engineers and innovators through experience and teamwork.
