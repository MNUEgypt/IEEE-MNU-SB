The story of cooperation between Mansoura National University and the IEEE began by approving by both parties the opening of the IEEE MNU SB on 1/5/2023
It is the first branch at the National universities are affiliated to government universities.

OUR COMMITTEES

HR Committee

The committee responsible for both recruitment and evaluation. They handle selecting new members based on specific criteria and evaluating team performance across different activities to ensure growth and quality within the branch.

PR Committee

The committee responsible for external communication.It handles speakers, presenters, partnerships, and media coverage by coordinating with newspapers, magazines, and journalists to promote the branch’s activities and events.

FR Committee

The committee responsible for securing sponsors and financial support for events. They use strong communication and persuasion skills to build partnerships and plan the strategic use of available financial resources.

Logistics Committee

The committee responsible for organizing and executing events. They manage logistics, preparation, and coordination to ensure every seminar or activity runs smoothly and represents the branch and university in the best way.

Coaching Committee

The committee responsible for organizing workshops and training programs across different fields. It helps members improve their skills in graphics, robotics, and other areas to enhance creativity and technical expertise within the branch.

Markting Committee

The committee responsible for marketing campaigns and creative ideas. It includes copywriters and content creators who plan and produce campaigns to reach the widest audience and promote the branch’s activities effectively.

Media Committee

The committee responsible for presenting the branch’s identity creatively. It includes graphic designers and video editors who design and edit visual content to showcase the branch attractively and ensure a strong, consistent image everywhere.
